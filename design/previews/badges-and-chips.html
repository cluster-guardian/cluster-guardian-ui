Always a glyph plus the word. Two of the severity colors sit below 3:1 on the light surface, so the pairing is what makes them legible — and what keeps them readable under color-vision deficiency. Never ship a severity as a bare colored dot.

Filter chip

A toggle, not a link — state lives in aria-pressed so it is announced, not just dimmed. Turning a severity off never repaints the others: color follows the level, never its rank in the current selection.
