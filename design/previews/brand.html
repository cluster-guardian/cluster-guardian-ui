Mark

A shield with a check: the tool guards a cluster and reports a verdict. Drawn on a 24px grid with a 1.8px stroke so it stays legible at 22px in the header and as a 16px favicon. The stroke is currentColor — the mark inherits ink and needs no per-theme variant.

App header

Sticky, one row, hairline rule. Auto-refresh is a switch rather than a default because a refresh in single-cluster mode triggers a real analysis against the API server. The Refresh button is absent entirely in fleet mode, where reports come from the scan schedule.

Cluster Guardian

Voice

Findings are stated, not sold: "2 containers running as root", never "Uh oh! We found some issues". Hints say what to change. Errors say what to run. Counts are exact, never "many". The tool is read-only and its output goes into CI and into leadership reports — the writing has to survive both.
