Severity — badge surfaces and text

Four levels, matching the backend's Severity enum: ok < info < warning < critical. Badge colors are tuned for a 12px uppercase label, which is a different contrast problem from a chart fill — that is why marks below are a separate set.

Severity — chart marks

A validated set, not a taste call. Light (on #ffffff): #d03b3b, #c98500, #2a78d6 — passes the lightness band, chroma floor, CVD separation and contrast checks. Dark (on #181b23): #e34948, #c98500, #3987e5 — passes, with critical↔warning at deutan ΔE 6.2, inside the 6–8 band. That is legal only with secondary encoding, which the trend chart supplies: 2px surface gaps between stacked fills, a legend, labelled tooltips and a table view.
