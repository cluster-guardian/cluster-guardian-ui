Faceted filter rail

Every facet value carries its result count, computed against the other facets. That is what makes the numbers trustworthy: each count is exactly how many results selecting it will produce, so a facet never advertises a number that clicking it cannot deliver. Severity is the exception — its counts ignore the severity selection, or turning one off would zero its own count and it could never be turned back on.

Selection shows as a checkbox glyph, not a color change: it has to be visible without relying on hue. A facet with no values is omitted entirely rather than rendered as an empty heading.
