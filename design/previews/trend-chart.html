Findings by severity over time

Form. Change over time where the parts sum to a meaningful whole (total findings) — a stacked area, on one y axis. Never a second axis for the score: two measures of different scale get two charts, not two scales.

Color. Severity is a status scale, not a categorical one, so the marks come from the validated status set rather than the series palette. Light passes every check clean. Dark passes with critical↔warning at deutan ΔE 6.2 — inside the 6–8 band, which is legal only with secondary encoding. Four things supply it here, and removing any of them means re-validating: the 2px surface-colored gap between stacked fills, the always-present legend, the labelled tooltip, and the table view.

Tooltip
