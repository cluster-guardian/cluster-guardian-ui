Type scale

One system sans throughout — no display or serif face. Numbers that must align vertically (table columns, axis ticks, counts) carry font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; standalone figures keep proportional digits.

2.4rem — hero figure --text-2xl
1.75rem — stat value --text-xl
1.3rem — page title --text-lg
1.05rem — section heading --text-md
0.95rem — body --text-base
0.85rem — secondary, hints --text-sm
0.72rem — badges, labels --text-xs
monospace — object refs, controls, server URLs --font-mono

Spacing

A 0.25rem base, doubling loosely. Gaps between cards are --space-4.

Radii & elevation

sm 8px for controls, md 12px for tiles, lg 16px for cards, pill for badges and chips. Shadows are hairline-plus-diffuse in light and a single flat drop in dark, where a border does more of the separating work.
